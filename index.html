WellWatch · early warnings for desert wells

Warns a village council weeks before a well runs dry, by plain SMS. Demo data is simulated (rainfall, vegetation index and well-level sensors), shaped like a Sahel/Arabian-fringe dry season.

1 · Pick a well

Line = water column above pump (m). Shaded = 80% confidence band. Red line = pump runs dry.

2 · False alarm vs missed alarm

Lower = warns earlier, more false alarms (trust cost). Higher = fewer false alarms, more missed wells (lives cost). Wells in dry spells are warned at a lower bar than usual because a miss is worse than a false alarm.
